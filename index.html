Cardboard Bot Flasher

Columbia Gadget Works · install or update the firmware on a robot's XIAO ESP32-C3 over USB.

This browser can't talk to USB serial devices. Use desktop Chrome or Edge, or Firefox 151 or later.

1 · Plug in the robot

Plug the XIAO into this computer with a USB-C data cable. The robot's power switch can be on or off. If the connect list shows nothing, hold the XIAO's B (boot) button while plugging it in.

2 · Firmware

Bootloader and partition table come from Stable.

3 · Robot settings

These go into the robot's settings area in the same pass. After you connect, the robot's current settings are filled in.

Defaults match the Cardboard Bot main board. Change them for a prototype or different wiring. Settings you don't change here are kept.

After installing

    
The robot restarts and the status light pulses blue. On a phone, join the robot's Wi-Fi
      (CGW- and its name), then open https://192.168.4.1 in Chrome or Safari. Accept the certificate warning once.
      Robots already running this firmware can also update themselves from the phone page (Update tab).
